Senior year just ended, and it feels pretty strange. I expected more people to come to the last couple days of school, but it seems everyone is busy. There's a bunch of senior events, like grad walk and senior sunset, going on,
but no one seems to want to come to those. 

I'm wondering how I should spend my time. All of high school, I've been trying to "double count" everything &mdash; my hobbies should help my future, relaxing should help me learn, hobbies should "have a purpose".
I used to do cubing, model rocketry, and quizbowl. Those were super fun, but honestly doing them became pretty performative. I was constantly grinding for schoolwork, clubs, or college apps. 
It seemed to work in my case, I got the decisions I wanted. But, I'm stuck here in a little bit of burnout. All of these things just don't seem fun anymore.

I've got a month before I start an internship. I wanna take it for myself, but if I fall into old habits I'm gonna end up working for the whole time. I think a good vacation should set me straight tho.

Senior events have been keeping me busy for now, but I actually need to learn to be bored again. It's the only way things will start being fun.
